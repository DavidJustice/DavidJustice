## Head of Software Development and Computer Science Teacher

For half the week I build the engineering that keeps a supermarket's technology arm ticking over. For the other half, I try to convince Year 9 that tabs versus spaces is a genuine moral question. It's not the mild-mannered engineer by day, secret teacher by night routine. Both jobs run at the same time. Strange way to spend a week.

Somewhere over the better part of a decade leading development teams I noticed that the graduates I managed were often more effective than people with twice their experience. Mostly because of how they worked with each other. That's most of why I ended up in a classroom. A room of fifteen-year-olds arguing about whose code is "more right" is closer to a stand-up than you'd expect.

**Nothing pinned below yet.** Most of what I build lives behind a corporate firewall or in a scheme of work, not a public repo. That'll change. It just hasn't yet.
